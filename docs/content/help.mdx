Tap a question to open it. Still stuck? **Settings → About and support → Report a problem** in the app (it adds your phone and version for you), or [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify/issues).

<Group title="Installing and updating">
<Fold q="Android won't let me install it">
  Totally normal for apps outside the Play Store. When Android asks, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser or Files app, go back, and tap **Install**. Says "unknown developer"? Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. Relaxify is free and its code is public.
</Fold>

<Fold q="How do I update without losing anything?">
  When a new update appears, you'll be notified in the app. Install it from there: it goes over the old version and keeps your playlists, likes and downloads. Never uninstall first, that deletes your playlists and likes for good.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Which version do I have?">
  **Settings → About and support** shows it as **App version**. [What's new](/releases) shows the newest one.
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Playing music">
<Fold q="The music stops when I leave the app">
  Your battery saver is being extra. Phone **Settings → Apps → Relaxify → Battery → Unrestricted** (some phones call it "Don't optimise" or "Allow background activity").
</Fold>

<Fold q="The music stops when I swipe the app away">
  That's on purpose: swiping Relaxify out of your recent apps means "close it". To keep listening, press **Home** or **Back** instead.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Songs keep stopping to load">
  Your internet is too slow for the quality you picked. Set **Settings → Streaming quality** to **Auto**: it plays a little lower for a while instead of stopping. [More](/sound#sound-quality)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A song skipped by itself">
  Relaxify tries other copies first and skips only when none will play (some songs are previews only, or blocked in your country). A short note says which. Search it again and pick one with a different source label.
</Fold>

<Fold q="The wrong version of a song plays">
  Tap the small source label under the song name and pick the right one. Relaxify remembers your pick everywhere. [How](/play#wrong-song)
</Fold>

<Fold q="Crossfade doesn't fade">
  Check **Settings → Crossfade** is above zero, and let songs end on their own (skipping never fades). The "end of song" sleep timer also turns it off.
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Finding and sharing">
<Fold q="Search shows nothing">
  You're probably offline: search needs the internet, and Home and Search say so when there's no connection. Your downloads still play: open **Downloads** in Your Library and tap **Play**. [More](/music#downloads)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A playlist import is stuck">
  Tap **** on the Home card. **Save** keeps what it found so far; **Discard** throws it away. If it says the playlist is private, make it public in Spotify or YouTube first. **Try again** starts over.
</Fold>

<Fold q="A friend's playlist link won't open">
  Update Relaxify first. If the link opens your browser instead, tap the playlist's name on that page. If the page shows no playlist name, your friend may have deleted the playlist.
</Fold>

<Fold q="My friend and I are a bit out of sync in a Jam">
  One of you is probably on Bluetooth, which adds a delay no app can see. Try phone speakers or wired headphones. [More](/together)
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Your data">
<Fold q="What does the app collect?">
  To see what works and fix what doesn't, Relaxify sends usage stats to Google's app statistics service (Firebase): songs played and for how long, searches, likes, playlists and downloads, which features you use, whether lyrics were found for a song, settings you change, errors and crashes, the app version, your phone model, and your rough location (city and country, from your internet connection). It's linked to an anonymous ID for your phone, never your name, number or contacts.

  Your Recap is made on your phone and sends nothing, and the artists you follow stay on your phone too. During a Jam, the song, where it's at and the names you enter are shared until it ends. A shared playlist keeps its name, songs and your name (your Jam name, or your phone's name) until you delete it. The full statement is in the app: **Settings → About and support → Privacy**.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where's everything in Settings?">
  - **Playback**: Autoplay, Normalize volume, Headphone memory, Resume when headphones connect, Crossfade, Equalizer.
  - **Sound**: Streaming quality and Data saver.
  - **Your music**: Home languages and Sources (YouTube on or off).
  - **Storage**: what's using space, your download folder, freeing space.
  - **Appearance**: show or hide the source and quality labels, and where each playlist came from.
  - **About and support**: your version, updates, what we collect, help.

  **Reset all settings** puts every setting back, and keeps your likes, playlists and downloads.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where does the music come from? Is it allowed?">
  Relaxify doesn't store any music. It plays what JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube already offer to everyone, the way their own websites do, for personal listening. It's free and open source: anyone can read the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify).
</Fold>
</Group>
