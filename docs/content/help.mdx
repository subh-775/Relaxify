Tap a question to open it. Still stuck? **[Report a problem](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify/issues)** on GitHub, or use **Report a problem** in the app's Settings, which fills in your version and phone for you.

<Group title="Playing">
<Fold q="A song skipped by itself">
  Before skipping, the app tries the song twice more: once with a fresh link, then by a slower route. It skips only when every copy it found fails, which happens with preview-only uploads or songs locked to some countries. The app tells you which song it skipped. Search for it again and pick a row with a different source label.
</Fold>

<Fold q="The music stops when I swipe the app away">
  That's on purpose: swiping Relaxify out of your recent apps means "close it", so the music stops and its notification goes. To keep listening, press **Home** or **Back** instead; the music carries on in the background.
</Fold>

<Fold q="The music stops when I leave the app">
  Your phone's battery saver closed it. Open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Relaxify → Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (some phones call it "Don't optimise" or "Allow background activity").
</Fold>

<Fold q='A note said "Weak signal"'>
  Your **Streaming quality** is on **Auto** and songs kept pausing to load, so the next ones stream at a lower quality to keep playing smoothly. It steps back up by itself after five smooth minutes. To always get one quality, pick it in **Settings → Streaming quality**. [More](/sound#quality-and-your-data)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A song went quiet, but the time kept going">
  The connection dropped for a moment. The play button shows a spinner while the song is loading, and after 10 seconds the app reconnects and carries on. If you're offline, it stops and says so; press play once you're back.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Crossfade doesn't fade">
  Check that **Settings → Crossfade** is above 0, that you're letting songs end by themselves (skipping never fades), and that the "end of song" sleep timer isn't on.
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Finding and keeping music">
<Fold q="Search is slow, or shows nothing">
  No results at all almost always means no internet: search needs a connection, downloads don't. If one source is slow, it's left out so the rest aren't held up; search again and it usually shows.
</Fold>

<Fold q="A downloaded song doesn't show as downloaded">
  Downloads are matched by their title and artist. If you edited those in another app, the match is lost. Download the song again.
</Fold>

<Fold q="The Spotify import is stuck">
  Tap **✕** on the Home card to stop it, then **Save** keeps the songs found so far as a playlist, or **Discard** throws them away. If the card says the playlist is private, make it public in Spotify, or tap **Another link**. **Try again** starts the whole import over from the first song.
</Fold>

<Fold q="My friend and I are slightly out of step in a Jam">
  Usually one of you is on Bluetooth, which adds a delay no app can see. Try the phone speakers or wired headphones. See [Together](/together).
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Updates and your data">
<Fold q="How do updates work?">
  The app checks GitHub when it starts and whenever you come back to it. When there's a new version, a green dot appears on the menu. **Settings → Check for updates** downloads it, checks the file arrived whole, and hands it to Android to install over the old one. Never uninstall first: that deletes your playlists and likes. **Settings → Automatic updates** turns the checks off.
</Fold>

<Fold q="What does the app collect?">
  To help improve the app, it sends usage statistics to Google (Firebase): songs played and for how long, searches, likes, playlists and downloads, settings you change, errors and crashes, the app version, your phone model and your rough location (country and city, from your internet address). It's tied to an anonymous ID for your phone, never your name, account or contacts. Your **Recap** is worked out on your phone and sends nothing. During a **Jam**, the song, its position, the shared queue and the names you enter are shared through a database until the Jam ends.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where is everything in Settings?">
  **Playback**: autoplay, normalize volume, headphone memory, resume when headphones connect, crossfade, equalizer. **Sound**: streaming quality, data saver. **Your music**: Home languages, weekly Recap. **Sources**: YouTube on or off. **Storage**: what's using space, download folder, removing all downloads, clearing the cache, and clearing it automatically once it passes a size you pick. **Appearance**: source and quality labels on songs. **About**: version, updates, what we collect, **Terms of Use**, **Privacy** and licences. **Reset all settings** puts everything back, and keeps your likes, playlists and downloads.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where does the music come from? Is this allowed?">
  Relaxify doesn't host any music. It plays what JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube already make public, the same way their own websites do, and it's for personal listening. Relaxify is free and open source under the **GPL-3.0** licence; the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify).
</Fold>
</Group>
