Answers to the questions people ask most. Tap a question to open it. If yours isn't here, use **Settings → About and support → Report a problem** in the app, which adds your phone model and app version for you, or [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify/issues).

<Group title="Installing and updating">
<Fold q="Android won't let me install it">
  This is normal for any app installed outside the Play Store. When Android asks, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser or Files app, go back and tap **Install**. If it warns about an unknown developer, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. Relaxify is free and its code is public for anyone to read.
</Fold>

<Fold q="How do I update without losing anything?">
  When a new version is ready, Relaxify shows it in the app by itself; there is nothing to check. Install it from there, or later from **Settings → About and support → Version**: it goes over the old version and keeps your playlists, likes and downloads. Don't uninstall first, because that deletes your playlists and likes for good.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Which version do I have?">
  **Settings → About and support** shows it as **Version**. [What's new](/releases) shows the newest one.
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Playing music">
<Fold q="The music stops when I leave the app">
  Your phone's battery saver is closing Relaxify in the background. Open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Relaxify → Battery** and choose **Unrestricted**. Some phones call it "Don't optimise" or "Allow background activity".
</Fold>

<Fold q="The music stops when I swipe the app away">
  That's intended: swiping Relaxify out of your recent apps closes it. To keep listening, press **Home** or **Back** instead.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Songs keep stopping to load">
  Your connection is too slow for the quality you chose. Set **Settings → Data saver and quality → Streaming quality** to **Auto**, which drops the quality for a while instead of stopping. [More about quality](/sound#sound-quality)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A song skipped by itself">
  Relaxify tries the other copies of a song first and only skips when none of them will play, for example when a song is a preview or isn't available in your country. A short message tells you when this happens. Search for the song again and pick a copy with a different source label.
</Fold>

<Fold q="The wrong version of a song plays">
  Tap the small source label under the song's name and choose the right copy. Relaxify remembers your choice everywhere. [How it works](/play#when-the-wrong-version-plays)
</Fold>

<Fold q="Crossfade doesn't fade">
  Check that **Settings → Playback → Crossfade** is above zero, and let songs end on their own: skipping never fades. The "end of song" sleep timer also turns crossfade off for that song.
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Finding and sharing">
<Fold q="Search shows nothing">
  You're probably offline. Search needs the internet, and Home and Search tell you when there's no connection. Your downloads still play: open **Downloads** in Your Library and tap **Play**. [Offline listening](/offline)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A playlist import is stuck">
  Tap **✕** on the Home card. **Save** keeps what was found so far and **Discard** throws it away. If it says the playlist is private, make it public in Spotify or YouTube first, then try again. [Bringing playlists over](/import)
</Fold>

<Fold q="A friend's playlist link won't open">
  Update Relaxify first. If the link opens in your browser instead, tap the playlist's name on that page. If the page shows no playlist name, your friend may have deleted the playlist.
</Fold>

<Fold q="My friend and I are slightly out of sync in a Jam">
  One of you is probably using Bluetooth, which adds a small delay that no app can measure. Try the phone's speaker or wired headphones. [Listening together](/together)
</Fold>
</Group>

<Group title="Your data">
<Fold q="What does the app collect?">
  To learn what works and fix what doesn't, Relaxify sends usage statistics to Google's app statistics service (Firebase): songs played and for how long, searches, likes, playlists and downloads, which features you use, whether lyrics were found for a song, settings you change, errors and crashes, the app version, your phone model, and your rough location (city and country, from your internet connection). It's linked to an anonymous ID for your phone, never to your name, number or contacts.

  Your Recap is made on your phone and sends nothing, and the artists you follow stay on your phone too. During a Jam, the song, its position and the names you enter are shared until the Jam ends. A shared playlist keeps its name, songs and your name (your Jam name, or your phone's name) until you delete it. The full statement is in the app under **Settings → About and support → Privacy**.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where is everything in Settings?">
  - **Playback**: Autoplay, Normalize volume, Headphone memory, Resume when headphones connect, Crossfade, the Equalizer and the Sleep timer.
  - **Data saver and quality**: Data saver on mobile data, and Streaming quality.
  - **Display preferences**: your languages for music, how Home is laid out, and the source, quality and playlist labels.
  - **Storage**: what is using space, your download folder, removing downloads and clearing the cache.
  - **About and support**: your version (and an update, when one is ready), the Privacy Policy, terms, licences and help.

  **Reset**, at the foot of Settings, puts every setting back to its default and keeps your likes, playlists and downloads.
</Fold>

<Fold q="Where does the music come from? Is it allowed?">
  Relaxify doesn't store any music. It plays what JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube already offer to everyone, the way their own websites do, for personal listening. It's free and open source, and anyone can read the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify).
</Fold>
</Group>
