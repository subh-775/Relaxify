Everything you keep is in **Your Library**: liked songs, your playlists, albums you saved, artists you follow, and downloads. Pin the ones you open most and they stay at the top. Inside a playlist or album, the search icon at the top finds a song in it.

## Likes and playlists

Tap **+** on any song and it goes into **Liked Songs**; the **+** turns into a tick. Tap the tick (or press and hold the **+**) to choose which playlists the song belongs in, or start a **New playlist** right there. Tapped the wrong thing? The note that pops up has **Undo**.

Every playlist is yours to change: rename it, give it a cover picture, reorder songs, or share it with friends. [Share a playlist](/together#share-a-playlist)

## Downloads

Tap the download button in the player, or **Download** in a song's menu, to save the song to your phone. You get a real music file with its cover, in your phone's **Download/Relaxify/music** folder. It plays without internet, other music apps can play it too, and it stays even if you ever remove Relaxify. Downloaded songs show a small download arrow next to them.

**Settings → Storage** shows how much space songs and the app take, and lets you clear space.

## Bring your playlists

Moving from Spotify or YouTube? Copy a **public** Spotify playlist or album link, or a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link, and paste it into the card on Home (or into Search). Relaxify reads the list, finds each song, and **saves the playlist to Your Library by itself**. You don't sign in to anything.

You can keep listening while it works; the Home card shows how far it has got. To stop part-way, tap **✕** on the card, then **Save** to keep the songs found so far, or **Discard**. If it takes unusually long, the card offers **Keep waiting** or **Cancel**. If it fails, **Try again** starts over.

Most songs are found. A few aren't, usually ones only released in some countries, or brand-new ones. From YouTube, a song Relaxify can't find elsewhere is kept as the YouTube original. Your own private YouTube lists (Liked music, Watch later) can't be read.

## Recents

**Recents** on Home shows songs you've been listening to. A song you tap goes in straight away. A song that played on its own (from Autoplay, or by skipping to it) goes in once you've listened to it for 30 seconds, so songs you skip past don't fill the list.

Tapping a song in Recents plays that song, with similar songs after it. To play your whole recent list in order, open the full list (the dotted tile) and tap **Play all**.

## Your Recap

Your listening, told as a short story of cards. Tap the Recap card on Home and tap through: how many songs you played and for how long, your top song and artist, the hour you listen most, your streak of days with music, and how this week compares with the last.

- A song counts once you've listened to it for **30 seconds**, so songs you always skip don't sneak into your top five.
- **This week** covers the last seven days; **All time** covers everything since you installed the app.
- On **Sunday**, a notification tells you your Recap is ready, if you allow Relaxify notifications. The Recap card is on Home on Sunday and Monday.
- On the last card, the **share** button sends a picture of your Recap to WhatsApp or anywhere else, with a link for friends to get the app.

Your Recap is worked out on your phone, from your own listening. Nothing is sent anywhere to make it.
