## Bring your Spotify playlists

<Banner pal="lime" art="stack" kicker="Moving from Spotify?" title="Paste a link, get a playlist">
  Copy a **public** Spotify playlist or album link, then paste it into the card on Home (or into Search). Relaxify reads the song list, finds each song on its own sources, and **saves the playlist to Your Library by itself**. No Spotify account needed.
</Banner>

You can keep browsing while it works; the Home card shows how far it got. Want to stop? Tap **✕** on the card. It then offers **Save** to keep the songs it found so far as a playlist, or **Discard** to throw them away. If an import stalls, the card says **Taking longer than usual**, with **Keep waiting** or **Cancel**. If it fails, **Try again** starts the whole import over from the first song.

Most songs match. A few won't, usually ones that only exist in some countries, or brand-new releases. The finished playlist is a normal one: rename it, reorder it, download it.

## Recap

Your listening, told as a story. Tap the Recap card on Home, and tap through the cards. Here's what it looks like:

<RecapFlip />

A song counts once you've listened for **30 seconds**, so songs you always skip don't sneak into your top five. **This week** and **All time** switch between the last seven days and everything since you installed the app.

On **Sunday evening** a notification sums up your week; tap it and your Recap opens. Don't want it? **Settings → Your music → Weekly Recap**.

Your Recap is worked out on your phone, from your own history. It doesn't send anything anywhere.
