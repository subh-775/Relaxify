Everything you keep lives in **Your Library**: liked songs, your playlists, saved albums, artists you follow, and downloads. Pin your favourites and they stay on top.

## Likes and playlists

Tap **+** on any song and it lands in **Liked Songs** (the **+** turns into a tick). Tap the tick, or hold the **+**, to pick which playlists it goes in, or start a **New playlist** right there. Tapped by mistake? The little note has **Undo**.

Every playlist is yours: rename it, give it a cover, reorder songs, or [share it with friends](/together#share-a-playlist).

## Downloads

Tap the download button in the player (or **Download** in a song's menu). You get a real music file with its cover, in your phone's **Download/Relaxify/music** folder. It plays without internet, works in other music apps, and stays even if you ever remove Relaxify. Downloaded songs show a small download arrow.

Open **Downloads** in Your Library and tap **Play** or **Shuffle**: your downloads play one after another and start again from the top when they run out (in a new random order if you shuffled). No internet needed. When there's no connection, Home and Search say so and point you here.

**Settings → Storage** shows what's taking space and lets you free some up.

## Artists you love

Tap **Follow** on an artist's page. Their newest songs and albums then show up on Home under **From artists you love**, newest first, and the artist stays in Your Library. Tap a single and it plays; tap an album and it opens.

## Bring your playlists

Moving from Spotify or YouTube? Copy a **public** Spotify playlist or album link, or a YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link, and paste it into the card on Home (or into Search). Relaxify finds every song and **saves the playlist for you**. No sign-in needed.

<Steps>
  <li>**Copy the link** in Spotify or YouTube (Share → Copy link).</li>
  <li>**Paste it** into the card on Home, or into Search.</li>
  <li>**Keep listening** while it works. The card shows how far it's got, and the playlist appears in Your Library when it's done.</li>
</Steps>

When the original playlist gets new songs, open your copy and tap the round **Sync** button next to Play and Shuffle: Relaxify finds the new ones and adds them. It never removes anything, so songs you took out stay out. Imported it before Sync existed? Use **⋮ → Sync with a Spotify or YouTube playlist** once and paste its link.

Want to stop halfway? Tap **** on the card, then **Save** to keep what it found so far, or **Discard**. Most songs get found; a few won't, usually brand-new or region-locked ones. Private YouTube lists (Liked music, Watch later) can't be read.

## Recents

**Recents** on Home shows what you've been playing. A song you tap goes in straight away; a song that played on its own (Autoplay, or skipping to it) goes in after 30 seconds of listening, so songs you skip past don't clutter it.

Tap a song in Recents and it plays with similar songs after it. Want the whole list in order? Open the full list (the dotted tile) and tap **Play all**.

## Your Recap

 Your week in music, told as a little story. Tap the Recap card on Home and tap through: how much you played, your top song and artist, the hour you listen most, your streak, and how this week compares with last week.

<Moves>
  <Move how="Counts after 30 seconds">Songs you always skip don't sneak into your top five.</Move>
  <Move how="This week or All time">The last seven days, or everything since you installed the app.</Move>
  <Move how="Sunday notification">Your Recap is ready on Sunday (if you allow notifications). The card stays on Home on Sunday and Monday.</Move>
  <Move how="Share it">The share button on the last card sends a picture of your Recap, with a link for friends to get the app.</Move>
</Moves>

Your Recap is made on your phone from your own listening. Nothing gets sent anywhere to make it.
