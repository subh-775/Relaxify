Everything you keep lives in **Your Library**: liked songs, your playlists, saved albums, the artists you follow and your downloads. Pin the ones you open most and they stay at the top.

## Likes and playlists

Tap **+** on any song to add it to **Liked Songs**; the **+** turns into a tick. Tap the tick to choose which playlists the song belongs in, or to start a new playlist right there.

Every playlist is yours to shape: rename it, give it a cover, reorder its songs, or [share it with a friend](/together#share-a-playlist).

## Follow the artists you love

Tap **Follow** on an artist's page. Their newest songs and albums then appear on Home under **From artists you love**, and the artist stays in Your Library.

## Your weekly Recap

Every Sunday, Relaxify puts together your week in music: how much you listened, your top song and artist, the hour you listen most, your listening streak, and how the week compares with the one before. Tap the Recap card on Home to go through it.

A song counts only after 30 seconds of listening, so songs you always skip don't sneak into your top five. Switch between **This week** and **All time** to see the bigger picture, and use the share button on the last card to send a picture of your Recap to friends.

Your Recap is made on your phone from your own listening. Nothing is sent anywhere to make it.
