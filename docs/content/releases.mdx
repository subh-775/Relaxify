Already have Relaxify? You don't need to download anything here: the app tells you when a new version is out, and **Settings → About and support → Check for updates** installs it over the one you have.

## New in 1.2.36

- **Share playlists as links.** A friend taps the link and the playlist opens in Relaxify, ready to play. **Add to library** keeps their own copy, and opening the link again won't add it twice.
- **Recommend Relaxify** in the menu sends friends one link that always gets the newest version.
- **Share your Recap** as a picture from its last card.
- **A new look:** a new logo, the app's own coral colour everywhere, and redesigned artist pages with the artist's photo across the top.
- **Smoother listening:** a song from Recents now plays on its own with similar songs after it, Shuffle never cuts off the song you're hearing, and swiping between songs is quicker.
- **The right face on artist pages.** Artist photos now come from the same place the songs do.

Every version, with the full list of changes, is on **[GitHub](https://github.com/subh-775/Relaxify/releases)**.
