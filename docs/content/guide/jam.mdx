# Jam

Listen to the same songs at the same moment as your friends, each on their own phone. Open the menu and tap **Jam**.

## Start or join

- **Start a Jam** turns what you are playing into a Jam and gives you a six-character code, like **K7QX2M**. **Share code** sends it through WhatsApp or anything else.
- **Join** takes that code. Your phone jumps to the same song, at the same second, playing or paused as the Jam is.

Everyone in a Jam needs Relaxify. Enter a name first; it is what the others see.

## Everyone controls it

Anyone in the Jam can play, pause, skip, pick a song or jump within one, and every phone follows within a couple of seconds.

**Add to Jam queue**, in any song's **⋮** menu, puts a song in the queue everyone shares. When a song ends, the Jam plays the shared queue first, then carries on from the phone that chose the last song.

## How it works

Each phone streams the music itself. Only the Jam's state travels between phones: which song, where in it, playing or paused, the shared queue, and the names. It goes through a small database, and the Jam is deleted when it ends.

The phones stay in step best with Relaxify open. With the screen off they carry on to the same next song together, and catch up on anything else (a skip, a pause) as soon as the app is open again.

<Note>
  A Jam ends when the person who started it taps **End the Jam for everyone**, or after six hours. Anyone else can **Leave the Jam** at any time.
</Note>
