# Recap

Your listening, told as a short story. Open the menu (swipe in from the left edge of Home, or tap the Relaxify mark at the top left) and tap **Recap**.

## What it shows

One card at a time:

1. **Your week**: how many songs you played and how long you listened.
2. **Your song**: the song you played most, with its cover.
3. **Your artist**: the artist you played most.
4. **Time listened**: minutes of music, with a bar for each day and your biggest day named.
5. **Your listening hour**: a clock of when you listen, and a name for it, like **Night owl** or **Early riser**.

A card is left out when there is nothing to show for it yet.

## Moving through it

- **Tap the right side** for the next card, **the left side** for the previous one.
- **Hold** anywhere to pause.
- Each card moves on by itself after a few seconds. The last one stays.
- **This week** and **All time** at the top switch between the last seven days and everything since you installed the app.

<Note>
  All time shows your most played song and artist since you installed Relaxify. Time listened and your listening hour come from the last seven days, because that is how long the app keeps a detailed history.
</Note>

## Where the numbers come from

Your own history on this phone. Recap works without a connection and sends nothing anywhere.

A song counts as played when it starts. Time listened counts only the part you actually heard, so skipping a song after five seconds adds five seconds.

Songs played before v1.2.24 count towards All time, but not towards this week's top song and artist.
