# Recap

Your listening, told as a short story. Open the menu (swipe in from the left edge of Home, or tap the Relaxify mark at the top left) and tap **Recap**.

## What it shows

One card at a time, each a bright page with its own artwork. The colours change every time you open Recap. Artists show with their own photo.

1. **Your week**: how many songs you played and how long you listened.
2. **Your song**: the song you played most, with its cover.
3. **Top songs**: your five most played songs.
4. **Your artist**: the artist you played most.
5. **Top artists**: your five most played artists.
6. **Time listened**: minutes of music, with a bar for each day and your biggest day named.
7. **Compared with last week**: how much more or less you listened than the week before.
8. **On repeat**: a song you played at least three times in one day.
9. **New to you**: artists you heard for the first time this week.
10. **Your listening hour**: a clock of when you listen, and a name for it, like **Night owl** or **Early riser**.
11. **Your streak**: how many days in a row you have played music, and your best run.
12. **Your recap in one picture**: everything above on one card.

A card is left out when there is nothing to show for it yet.

## Moving through it

- **Tap the right side** for the next card, **the left side** for the previous one.
- **Hold** anywhere to pause.
- **Swipe down** (or press back) to close Recap. The music keeps playing.
- Each card moves on by itself after a few seconds. The last one stays.
- **This week** and **All time** at the top switch between the last seven days and everything since you installed the app.

<Note>
  All time shows your most played songs and artists since you installed Relaxify. Time listened and your listening hour come from the last seven days, and the comparison from the seven days before, because that is how long the app keeps a detailed history.
</Note>

## Where the numbers come from

Your own history on this phone. Recap works without a connection and sends nothing anywhere.

A song counts as played once you have listened to it for 30 seconds (half of it, for a song shorter than a minute), so a song you always skip does not become your top song. Time listened counts only the part you actually heard.

Songs played before v1.2.24 count towards All time, but not towards this week's top songs and artists. The comparison with last week, new artists and your best streak start filling in from v1.2.25.

## The Sunday notification

On Sunday evening, around 7 pm, Relaxify sends one notification with how many songs you played that week and your top song. Tap it to open this week's Recap. A week with no music sends nothing. Turn it off in **Settings → Your music → Weekly Recap**.
