Switching music apps usually means starting your playlists from scratch. With Relaxify you don't have to. Give it a link to a playlist from Spotify, YouTube or YouTube Music, and it rebuilds the whole thing for you, song by song. No sign-in to the other app is needed.

## Bring a playlist over

<Steps>
  <li>**Copy the link.** In Spotify or YouTube, open the playlist and tap **Share → Copy link**. Spotify albums work too.</li>
  <li>**Paste it into Relaxify**, either into the **Bring your playlists** card on Home or straight into Search.</li>
  <li>**Keep listening while it works.** The card shows how far it has got, and the playlist appears in Your Library when it's done.</li>
</Steps>

Most songs are found. The few that aren't are usually brand-new releases or songs that aren't available in your country.

The playlist has to be public. Private YouTube lists such as Liked music or Watch later can't be read by any app other than YouTube.

## Keep it in step with the original

When the original playlist gets new songs, open your copy and tap the round **Sync** button next to Play and Shuffle. Relaxify adds the new songs and never removes any, so a song you took out stays out.

An imported playlist shows where it came from (**Spotify**, **YT Music** or **YouTube**) under its name. If you'd rather not see that, turn off **Settings → Appearance → Show playlist source**.

## Stop an import

Tap **✕** on the Home card. **Save** keeps the songs found so far as a playlist, and **Discard** throws them away.
