Music is better shared. With a Jam, you and your friends hear the same song at the same moment, each on your own phone, whether you're in the same room or in different cities.

## Start a Jam

Everyone needs Relaxify installed. Open the menu and tap **Jam**.

<Steps>
  <li>**Start a Jam.** You get a six-character code, such as **K7QX2M**. Send it to your friends.</li>
  <li>**Your friends join with the code.** Their phones jump to the same song at the same second, playing or paused, just like yours.</li>
  <li>**Everyone can pick the music.** Anyone can play, pause, skip or choose a song, and every phone follows.</li>
</Steps>

To line up songs for the group, choose **Add to Jam queue** from any song's menu. When a song ends, the Jam plays from that queue first and then carries on with similar songs.

You can add a name so friends know who joined; without one, they see your phone's model. A Jam ends when the person who started it taps **End the Jam for everyone**, or after six hours.

Bluetooth earbuds and speakers play slightly late, often by about a fifth of a second, and no app can measure that delay. For the tightest Jam, use your phone's speaker or wired headphones.

## Share a playlist

Not listening at the same time? Share a playlist instead. Open one of your playlists, tap the share button at the top and choose where to send it. Your friend gets the playlist's name and a link.

If they have Relaxify, the link opens the playlist in the app, ready to play, and **Add to library** saves their own copy. If they don't have it yet, the link shows the playlist's name and a button to install Relaxify.

Their copy is their own: changes they make don't touch your playlist, and yours don't touch theirs. If you delete the playlist, the link stops working, but copies already saved stay.

## What gets shared

In a Jam, the phones exchange only the song, the position in it, whether it's playing, the shared queue and the names you enter. No audio is sent; each phone plays the music itself. A shared playlist keeps its name, its songs and the name you share it under until you delete it.
