A **Jam** lets you and your friends hear the same song at the same moment, each on your own phone, wherever you are. Open the menu and tap **Jam**.

<Row>
  <Banner pal="lav" art="dots" kicker="Start one" title="Share a code">
    **Start a Jam** gives you a six-character code like **K7QX2M**. Send it on WhatsApp or anywhere.
  </Banner>
  <Banner pal="teal" kicker="Join one" title="Type the code">
    Your phone jumps to the same song, at the same second, playing or paused, just like theirs.
  </Banner>
</Row>

Everyone needs Relaxify. Adding a name is optional; without one, friends see your phone's model.

## Everyone's the DJ

Anyone in the Jam can play, pause, skip, pick a song or jump within one, and every phone follows. **Add to Jam queue** (in any song's **⋮** menu) puts a song in the queue everyone shares. When a song ends, the shared queue goes first, then the Jam keeps going with similar songs.

## How close is "together"?

Very. Every phone checks itself against the Jam every couple of seconds. A phone that's a hair ahead or behind plays a few percent slower or faster for a moment until it's back in step, and you can't hear it happen. A phone that's way off jumps straight to the right spot.

<Banner pal="night" kicker="The one gap no app can see" title="Bluetooth adds its own delay">
  Wireless earbuds and speakers take a moment to play what they receive, often about a fifth of a second. For the tightest Jam, use your phones' own speakers, or wired headphones on both.
</Banner>

## Share a playlist

Not listening at the same time? Share a playlist instead. Open one of your playlists and tap **Share** at the top.

<Row>
  <Banner pal="orange" kicker="On your phone" title="A code to send">
    You get a six-character code and a ready-to-send message. **Send…** hands it to WhatsApp or anything else. **Shared by** is the name friends see.
  </Banner>
  <Banner pal="pink" kicker="On theirs" title="It keeps up with yours">
    They tap the **ticket** in Your Library and enter the code, or paste your whole message into Search. Songs you add or remove reach them by themselves, marked **New**.
  </Banner>
</Row>

Only you can change it. They can play it, download it, or **Save a copy** to make their own. If you **Stop sharing** or delete it, they keep it as an ordinary playlist of theirs.

## What a Jam shares

Only the song, where it is, playing or paused, the shared queue and the names you enter. No audio travels between phones; each one streams the music itself. A Jam can only be opened with its code, and it's deleted when it ends: when whoever started it taps **End the Jam for everyone**, or after six hours.

A shared playlist keeps its name, its songs and the name you share it under in the same database, for anyone with its code, until you stop sharing it.
