## Jam: same song, same second

A **Jam** lets you and your friends hear the same song at the same moment, each on your own phone, wherever you are. Everyone needs Relaxify. Open the menu and tap **Jam**.

<Steps>
  <li>**Start a Jam.** You get a six-character code, like **K7QX2M**. Drop it in the group chat.</li>
  <li>**Friends join with the code.** Their phone jumps to the same song, same second, playing or paused, just like yours.</li>
  <li>**Everyone's the DJ.** Anyone can play, pause, skip or pick a song, and every phone follows.</li>
</Steps>

Adding a name is optional (without one, friends see your phone's model). Want to line up songs for everyone? Use **Add to Jam queue** in any song's **⋮** menu. When a song ends, the Jam plays from that queue first, then keeps going with similar songs.

<Callout title="Bluetooth heads-up">
  Wireless earbuds and speakers play a tiny bit late (often about a fifth of a second), and no app can see that delay. For the tightest Jam, use phone speakers or wired headphones.
</Callout>

A Jam ends when whoever started it taps **End the Jam for everyone**, or after six hours.

## Share a playlist

 Not listening at the same time? Share a playlist instead. Open one of yours, tap the share button at the top, and send the link anywhere.

<Moves>
  <Move how="Friend has Relaxify">The playlist opens right in the app, ready to play. **Add to library** keeps their own copy. Open the link again and it says **In your library**, no duplicates.</Move>
  <Move how="Friend doesn't have it yet">The link takes them to the download page first.</Move>
</Moves>

The copy a friend adds is theirs: their changes don't touch your playlist and yours don't touch theirs. Anyone opening your link later sees your playlist as it is then. The link carries a six-character code too: typed into Search, it opens the playlist the same way. Delete the playlist and the link stops working; copies already added stay.

## Tell a friend

Menu → **Recommend Relaxify** sends a short message with a link that always gets the newest version. One link, forever. No need to send a new one every update.

## What gets shared

In a Jam, only the song, where it's at, whether it's playing, the shared queue and the names you enter go between phones. No sound is sent: each phone plays the music itself. A shared playlist keeps its name, songs and the name you share it under, so anyone with the link can open it, until you delete the playlist.
