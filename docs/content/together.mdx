## Jam: the same song, at the same moment

A **Jam** lets you and your friends hear the same song at the same second, each on your own phone, wherever you are. Everyone needs Relaxify. Open the menu and tap **Jam**.

<Steps>
  <li>**Start a Jam.** You get a six-character code, like **K7QX2M**. Send it to your friends on WhatsApp or anywhere.</li>
  <li>**Friends join with the code.** Their phone jumps to the same song, at the same second, playing or paused, just like yours.</li>
  <li>**Everyone can choose.** Anyone in the Jam can play, pause, skip, pick a song or jump within one, and every phone follows.</li>
</Steps>

Adding your name is optional; without one, friends see your phone's model. To line up songs for everyone, use **Add to Jam queue** in any song's **⋮** menu. When a song ends, the Jam plays from that queue first, then keeps going with similar songs.

### How close is "the same moment"?

Very close. Every phone checks itself against the Jam every couple of seconds and quietly corrects itself, so you can't hear it happen.

<Callout title="Bluetooth adds a small delay">
  Wireless earbuds and speakers play a little after your phone sends the sound, often about a fifth of a second, and no app can see that delay. For the tightest Jam, use phone speakers or wired headphones.
</Callout>

A Jam ends when the person who started it taps **End the Jam for everyone**, or after six hours.

## Share a playlist

Not listening at the same time? Share a playlist instead. Open one of your playlists and tap the share button at the top. You get a link and a ready-made message; **Send…** passes it to WhatsApp or any app.

When a friend taps the link:

<Moves>
  <Move how="They have Relaxify">The playlist opens in the app, ready to play. **Add to library** keeps a copy in their Library. If they open the link again, it says **In your library** instead of adding it twice.</Move>
  <Move how="They don't have it yet">The link takes them to the download page first.</Move>
</Moves>

The copy a friend adds is theirs: songs they add or remove don't change your playlist, and your later changes don't change theirs. Anyone who opens your link later sees your playlist as it is then.

The message also carries a six-character code, for typing in by hand: in Your Library, tap the ticket icon and enter it. If you tap **Stop sharing** or delete the playlist, the link stops working; copies friends already added stay with them.

## Tell a friend about Relaxify

Open the menu and tap **Recommend Relaxify**. It sends a short message with a link that always gets the newest version of the app, so you never need to send a new link when Relaxify updates.

## What gets shared

During a Jam, only the song, where it is, whether it's playing, the shared queue and the names people enter pass between phones. No sound is sent between phones: each one plays the music itself. A shared playlist keeps its name, its songs and the name you share it under, so anyone with the link can open it, until you stop sharing it.
