Relaxify plays music from JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube, all in one app. You search once, it finds the song wherever it lives, and plays the best copy. No account. No ads. That's the whole deal.

## Get it on your phone

It's not on the Play Store, so you install the file yourself. Takes about a minute, promise.

<Steps>
  <li>
    **Install on your phone.**

    <GetApp />
</li>
  <li>**Open the file** from the notification, or from your Downloads folder.</li>
  <li>**Tap Install.** First time, Android asks if your browser can install apps. Turn it on for that one, go back, tap Install. Says "unknown developer"? Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.</li>
  <li>**Pick your languages.** Up to three. Home fills up with songs, charts and playlists in them. Done!</li>
</Steps>

You need Android 8 or newer and about 120 MB free. That's it.

## Updating

When a new update appears, you'll be notified in the app. Install it from there and it goes over the version you have, keeping everything.

<Callout kind="important" title="Never uninstall to update">
  Removing the app deletes your playlists, likes and history, and there's no getting them back. Always update from inside the app. Your downloaded songs are safe either way: they live in your phone's music folder.
</Callout>
