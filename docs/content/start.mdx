Relaxify is a free music app for Android. You search once, it looks on JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube for you, and plays the best copy it finds. No account, no sign-in, no ads.

## Get it on your phone

<Steps>
  <li>
    **Download the app.** Tap the button below on your phone. It's one file, straight from GitHub.

    <GetApp />
  </li>
  <li>
    **Let Android install it.** The first time, Android asks whether your browser (or Files) may install apps. Say yes for that one app, go back, and the install carries on.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Open it and pick your languages.** Three quick cards show what the app does, then you choose the languages Home should play from. Skip it if you like; you can change them later in Settings.
  </li>
</Steps>

You need Android 8 or newer and about 120 MB of space. That's it.

## Updating, without losing anything

The app tells you when a new version is out: a green dot appears on the menu, and **Settings → Check for updates** installs it over the old one.

<Banner pal="night" kicker="The one rule" title="Never uninstall to update">
  Uninstalling wipes your playlists, likes and history, and Android can't bring them back. Always install the new version **over** the old one; the in-app updater does exactly that. Your downloaded songs are safe either way, they live in your own music folder.
</Banner>
