Relaxify plays music from JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube in one app. You search once, it finds the song wherever it is, and plays the best copy. You don't make an account, and there are no ads.

## Install it

Relaxify isn't on the Play Store, so you install it from a file. It takes about a minute.

<Steps>
  <li>
    **Download the app on your phone.** It's one file, always the newest version.

    <GetApp />
  </li>
  <li>
    **Open the file** when the download finishes, from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Allow the install.** The first time, Android asks whether your browser (or Files app) may install apps. Turn it on for that one app, go back, and the install carries on. If Android says the app is from an unknown developer, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
  </li>
  <li>
    **Open Relaxify and choose your languages.** Pick up to three. Home fills with songs, charts and playlists in them. You can change them later from the menu, under **Home languages**.
  </li>
</Steps>

You need a phone with Android 8 or newer and about 120 MB of free space.

## Your Home screen

At the top, swipe between four cards:

<Moves>
  <Move how="Recap">Your week in music: songs you played most, your top artist, the hour you listen most. It shows on Sunday and Monday. [More about Recap](/music#your-recap)</Move>
  <Move how="Jam">Listen to the same song at the same moment as your friends. [How Jam works](/together)</Move>
  <Move how="Bring your playlists">Paste a Spotify or YouTube playlist link and Relaxify makes the same playlist. [How it works](/music#bring-your-playlists)</Move>
  <Move how="Continue">The last playlist or album you played, ready to carry on. On your first day it offers a chart in your languages.</Move>
</Moves>

Below the cards, **Recents** shows the songs you've listened to lately; tap one to hear it again. Then come trending songs, new releases, charts and playlists, all in your languages.

## The menu

Tap the Relaxify mark at the top-left of Home, Search or Your Library, or swipe right on an empty part of Home. It has:

- **Equalizer**: shape the sound. [Sound](/sound)
- **Jam**: listen with friends. [Listen together](/together)
- **How to use**: opens these pages.
- **Home languages**: change the languages Home plays from.
- **Recommend Relaxify**: send a friend a link to get the app.
- **Settings**: everything else.

## Keeping it up to date

Relaxify checks for new versions by itself. When one is out, a dot appears on the menu, and **Settings → About and support → Check for updates** installs it over the version you have.

<Callout kind="important" title="Never uninstall to update">
  Removing the app deletes your playlists, likes and listening history, and there's no way to get them back. Always update from inside the app. Songs you downloaded are safe either way: they're in your phone's music folder.
</Callout>
