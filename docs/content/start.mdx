Relaxify is a free music app for Android. You search for a song once, and it looks across JioSaavn, SoundCloud and, if you like, YouTube, then plays the best copy it finds. There is no account to create, no ads between songs and nothing to pay.

This page gets you from download to your first song. It takes about a minute.

## Install the app

Relaxify isn't on the Play Store, so you download the app file and install it yourself. Android asks a couple of questions the first time; here is what to tap.

<Steps>
  <li>
    **Download the app.**

    <GetApp />
</li>
  <li>**Open the file**, from the download notification or your Downloads folder.</li>
  <li>**Tap Install.** The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps. Allow it for that browser, go back and tap Install again. If you see an "unknown developer" warning, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. It appears for any app installed outside the Play Store.</li>
  <li>**Choose your languages.** Pick up to two, and Home fills with songs, charts and playlists in them.</li>
</Steps>

You need Android 8 or newer and about 120 MB of free space.

## What you'll find on Home

Home is built around what you listen to. At the top is **Discover**, nine songs picked for you each time you open the app, next to **Recents**, the songs you played lately. Further down are trending songs, new releases, the latest from artists you follow, and charts and playlists in your languages.

You can change your languages whenever you like: open the menu (the Relaxify logo at the top left) and tap **Languages for music**. Pick up to two; the rest wait until you turn one off.

## Keeping it up to date

When a new version is ready, Relaxify tells you inside the app. Install it from there and it goes over the version you have, keeping your playlists, likes and downloads.

<Callout kind="important" title="Don't uninstall to update">
  Uninstalling deletes your playlists, likes and listening history, and they can't be brought back. Always update from inside the app. Downloaded songs are safe either way, because they live in your phone's music folder.
</Callout>
