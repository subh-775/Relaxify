Some of the best listening happens where the signal doesn't reach: on a flight, on a long train ride, in a basement gym. Download the songs you love and they play anywhere, with no internet and no data used.

## Download a song

Open a song in the player and tap the download button next to its name, or choose **Download** from the song's menu. A small arrow marks every song you have downloaded.

Each download is a real music file with its cover art, saved to your phone's **Download/Relaxify/music** folder. Downloads always come in the best quality the song has, whatever streaming quality you use.

## Play without internet

Open **Downloads** in Your Library and tap **Play** or **Shuffle**. Your downloads play one after another and start again from the top when they run out, in a new order if you shuffled.

If you lose your connection, Home and Search tell you so and point you to your downloads, so the music doesn't have to stop.

## Your files are yours

Because downloads are ordinary music files in a normal folder, they also play in other music apps, and they stay on your phone even if you remove Relaxify one day.

To see how much space your music takes, or to clear some, open **Settings → Storage**.
