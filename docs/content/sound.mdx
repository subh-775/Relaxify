## The equalizer

Menu → **Equalizer**. Turn it on, pick a vibe, done: **Flat, Rock, Metal, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Classical, Jazz, Vocal, Bass Boost** or **Treble Boost**. Drag any of the eight sliders (bass on the left, treble on the right) and it becomes your own **Custom** sound. Off means music plays exactly as recorded.

If it asks you to play something first, start a song: it works on whatever's playing.

<Moves>
  <Move how="Headphone memory">Each pair of headphones, speaker or car keeps its own sound. Extra bass for your earbuds, flatter for the car, and it switches the moment they connect. **Settings → Playback**.</Move>
  <Move how="Normalize volume">Every song plays at about the same loudness, so a quiet track isn't followed by a super loud one. **Settings → Playback**.</Move>

## Crossfade

**Settings → Crossfade** blends the end of one song into the next, up to 12 seconds. Zero means off, which suits albums where songs flow into each other. Works with the screen off too. Skipping yourself never fades.

## Sound quality

**Settings → Streaming quality**:

| Pick | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Auto | The best each song has. On a weak signal it plays a little lower for a while instead of stopping. |
| Low | Uses the least data, about 43 MB an hour. |
| Normal | A good balance. |
| High | Clearer sound, more data. |
| Very High | The best sound, the most data. |

Downloads always get the best quality. The small label under the song name in the player shows what you're getting right now.

## Saving mobile data

**Data saver on mobile data** is on unless you switch it off (**Settings → Sound**). Away from Wi-Fi, songs play at Low quality, about 43 MB an hour. Back on Wi-Fi, full quality is back.
