Every song is mixed a little differently, and every pair of headphones hears it a little differently again. The equalizer lets you bring out what you care about: a deeper bass line, a clearer vocal, the shimmer of a guitar.

## The equalizer

Open the menu and tap **Equalizer**. Turn it on and choose a starting point: **Flat, Rock, Metal, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Classical, Jazz, Vocal, Bass Boost** or **Treble Boost**.

Then make it yours. There are eight sliders, from deep bass on the left to the airy top end on the right. Raise the low ones to feel the kick drum and bass, the middle ones to bring a voice or a guitar forward, the high ones for cymbals and detail. As soon as you move a slider, it is saved as your own **Custom** sound. Turn the equalizer off to hear the music exactly as it was recorded.

The equalizer works on whatever is playing, so if it asks you to play something first, start a song.

## A sound for each pair of headphones

With **Headphone memory** on, every pair of headphones, speaker and car stereo keeps its own equalizer setting: more bass for your earbuds, something flatter for the car. Relaxify switches the moment they connect. Find it in **Settings → Playback**.

In the same place, **Normalize volume** plays every song at about the same loudness, so a quiet track isn't followed by a sudden loud one.

## Crossfade

**Settings → Playback → Crossfade** blends the end of one song into the start of the next, by up to 12 seconds. Set it to zero for albums where songs flow into each other. Songs you skip yourself always change straight away.

## Sound quality and data

Choose how songs stream in **Settings → Data saver and quality → Streaming quality**:

| Setting | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Auto | The best each song has. On a weak signal it drops a little for a while instead of stopping. |
| Low | The least data, about 43 MB an hour. |
| Normal | A good balance of sound and data. |
| High | Clearer sound, more data. |
| Very High | The best sound and the most data. |

**Data saver on mobile data** is on unless you turn it off. Away from Wi-Fi, songs play at Low quality; back on Wi-Fi, full quality returns. It's in **Settings → Data saver and quality**.
