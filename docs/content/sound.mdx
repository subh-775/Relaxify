## The equalizer

Open the menu and tap **Equalizer**. Turn it on, then pick a starting point: **Flat, Rock, Metal, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Classical, Jazz, Vocal, Bass Boost** or **Treble Boost**. Drag any of the eight sliders, from deep bass on the left to crisp treble on the right, and it becomes your own **Custom** sound. With the equalizer off, music plays exactly as recorded.

If the equalizer asks you to play something first, start a song: it works on the music that's playing.

### Each pair of headphones keeps its own sound

With **Headphone memory** on (**Settings → Playback**), Relaxify remembers your equalizer for each pair of headphones, speaker or car. Set more bass for your earbuds and something flatter for the car, and it switches the moment they connect.

### Even volume

**Normalize volume** (**Settings → Playback**) plays every song at about the same loudness, so a quiet song isn't followed by a very loud one.

## Crossfade

**Settings → Crossfade** blends the end of one song into the start of the next, for up to 12 seconds. At zero it's off, which suits albums where songs run into each other. It also works with the screen off. Skipping a song yourself never fades.

## Sound quality

**Settings → Streaming quality** sets the best quality you want:

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Auto | The best each song has, and a little lower for a while when your signal is weak, so the music doesn't keep stopping to load. |
| Low | Uses the least data, about 43 MB an hour. |
| Normal | A balance of sound and data. |
| High | Clearer sound, more data. |
| Very High | The best sound, the most data. |

Downloads always get the best quality available. The small label under the song name in the player shows the quality you're getting right now.

## Saving mobile data

**Data saver on mobile data** is on unless you turn it off (**Settings → Sound**). Away from Wi-Fi, songs play at **Low** quality, about 43 MB an hour. Back on Wi-Fi, you get your chosen quality again.
