## The equalizer

Open it from the menu. Eight bands, from deep bass to sparkle, and eleven presets to start from: **Flat, Rock, Metal, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Classical, Jazz, Vocal, Bass Boost** and **Treble Boost**. Drag any band and it becomes your own **Custom** mix, starting from the preset you had.

<Row>
  <Banner pal="orange" art="bars" kicker="Headphone memory" title="Each pair keeps its own sound">
    Set a bassy mix for your earbuds and a flatter one for the car. Relaxify remembers which is which and switches the moment they connect.
  </Banner>
  <Banner pal="lav" kicker="Normalize volume" title="No more volume jumps">
    Plays every song at about the same loudness, so a quiet track isn't followed by a shouty one.
  </Banner>
</Row>

Your phone decides how many real bands it has (usually five), so Relaxify spreads your eight across them. If the equalizer asks you to play something first, start a song: it attaches to the music that's playing.

## Crossfade

**Settings → Crossfade** blends the end of one song into the start of the next, from 0 to 12 seconds. Zero is off, which suits albums where songs run into each other. It works with the screen off too. Skipping by hand never fades.

## Quality, and your data

**Settings → Streaming quality** sets the most you want: Low (96 kbps), Normal (128), High (256), Very High (320), or **Auto** for the best each source has. Downloads always get the best quality available.

<Banner pal="teal" kicker="On by default" title="Data saver on mobile data">
  Off Wi-Fi, Relaxify streams at 96 kbps, about 43 MB an hour. Back on Wi-Fi, you get your full quality again. Turn it off in **Settings → Sound**.
</Banner>

The small label under the song name in the player shows the quality you're actually getting.
