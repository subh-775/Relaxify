# Settings

Open the menu (swipe in from the left edge of Home, or tap the Relaxify mark at the top left of Home, Search or Your Library), then tap **Settings**.

## The menu

| Entry | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Equalizer** | Eight bands and eleven presets. See [Equalizer](/guide/equalizer) |
| **Recap** | Your listening as a short story: top song, top artist, time listened, your listening hour. See [Recap](/guide/recap) |
| **How to use** | This site, in your browser |
| **Settings** | Everything below |

## Playback

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Crossfade** | Opens the Playback screen. Shows the current crossfade, or Off |
| **Equalizer** | Opens the equalizer. Shows the preset, or Off |

### The Playback screen

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Autoplay** | On | Adds similar songs when the queue runs low. See [The Queue](/guide/queue) |
| **Normalize volume** | Off | Plays every song at about the same loudness |
| **Crossfade** | Off | Fades one song into the next, 0 to 12 seconds |
| **Sleep timer** | Off | Stops the music later. See below |

Turning Autoplay off also removes the suggested songs already waiting in the queue.

### Crossfade

Drag or tap the bar. It moves in whole seconds. 0 is off, which suits albums where songs run straight into each other. See [Sound & Quality](/reference/sound#crossfade).

### Sleep timer

Stop after **15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes**, or at the **end of this track**. You can also set it from the timer button at the bottom left of the full player. It turns green while a timer is set.

A timed stop **fades out** over a couple of seconds instead of stopping suddenly.

**End of track** stops on the song's last second, even with the screen off. The next song does not start. Press play later and it carries on from there. Pressing next does **not** stop the music. The timer waits for a song to end by itself.

## Audio quality

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Streaming quality** | Auto | Auto, Low (96), Normal (128), High (256) or Very High (320 kbps) |

Tap the row to choose from a sheet. The setting is an **upper limit**. If a source does not have that bitrate, you get its best. The label under the song name in the full player shows what you actually got. If the source does not say, there is no label.

Downloads always use the best quality a source has, whatever this is set to.

## Content sources

| Source | Switch |
| --- | --- |
| **JioSaavn** | Always on (locked) |
| **SoundCloud** | Always on (locked) |
| **YouTube** | On or off |

JioSaavn and SoundCloud cannot be turned off. They are searched together, and each is the backup when the other cannot play a song. YouTube is searched with them when it is on.

YouTube protects its streams, and the app has to decode each one before it can play it. Turning YouTube on first tests this by opening one YouTube stream on your phone, and the switch turns on only if the test succeeds. If it stays off, the test failed on your phone; YouTube changes its protection from time to time, so trying again after an update can work.

## Downloads

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Download location** | Where files are saved: **Download/Relaxify/music** unless you pick another folder. Tap to change it |
| **Open in Files** | Opens that folder in your file manager |
| **Clear cached data** | Removes temporary files, saved lyrics and search history. Shows how much it frees |
| **Clear cache automatically** | Off, or 10 to 100 MB (100 MB to start with). When the cache grows past it, it is cleared the next time you open the app |

**Clearing the cache never touches downloads, playlists or liked songs.** The automatic clear also keeps your search history.

Songs from older versions, saved in **Download/Fix_Spotify/music**, are moved to **Download/Relaxify/music** the first time v1.2.24 or newer starts. The old folder is removed once it is empty.

## Usage statistics

**What Relaxify collects** lists what the app sends to help improve it. There is no switch; this is the notice. See [Data & Storage](/reference/architecture#usage-statistics).

## Reset all settings

Puts every setting back to its default. **Your likes, playlists and downloads are kept.**
