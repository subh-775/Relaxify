## One search, every source

Type a song, an artist, an album, or a line you remember from the lyrics. Relaxify searches JioSaavn, SoundCloud and YouTube at the same time. When the same song turns up in more than one place, you see it once; the other copies are kept as backups in case one won't play.

Search also understands links. Paste a Spotify, YouTube or YouTube Music playlist link and it becomes a Relaxify playlist ([more on that](/import)); paste a friend's Relaxify playlist link and it opens.

## When the wrong version plays

Sometimes a search lands on a slowed-down edit, a cover, or a different song with the same name. In the player, tap the small source label under the song's name, or choose **Wrong song?** from the song's menu.

You'll see the copy playing now, the other copies Relaxify found, and a search box. Tap one to try it; it starts from the same second. Tap **Use this one** and Relaxify remembers your choice everywhere that song appears, in every playlist and album.

## Lyrics

Tap **Lyrics** in the player. The line being sung is highlighted as the song plays, and tapping any line jumps there. Most popular songs have lyrics. Some have the words without timings; those show as plain text marked **Lyrics not timed**.

## Music that keeps going

When your queue is about to run out, **Autoplay** adds songs like the one you're hearing, a different mix each time. Starting a song from Search or from Home's Discover and Recents works the same way: similar songs follow. If you'd rather the music stop at the end of your queue, turn off **Settings → Autoplay**.

## Speed and the sleep timer

The speed control plays anything from a quarter speed to twice as fast without changing the pitch of voices, which is handy for podcasts and practice. The sleep timer stops the music after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or at the end of the current song, fading out gently so it doesn't wake you.
