## One search, everywhere

Type a song, an artist or a line you remember. Relaxify asks JioSaavn and SoundCloud at the same time, and YouTube too if you switch it on in **Settings → Sources**. A song found in several places shows up once, with the others kept as backups: if one copy won't play, the next one does.

Each result carries a small label saying where it came from, so you can pick another copy if one sounds off.

<Banner pal="lime" art="search" kicker="Paste a link instead" title="Playlist links work too">
  Paste a **Spotify**, **YouTube** or **YouTube Music** playlist link into Search and Relaxify imports it as a playlist. A friend's share message works too. [How it works](/music#bring-your-playlists)
</Banner>

## The player, with one thumb

Tap the mini player at the bottom to open the full player. Everything below also has a button; the gestures are just quicker.

<Row>
  <Banner pal="coral" kicker="Swipe the cover" title="Next or previous">
    Swipe left for the next song, right for the one before. Let go early and nothing changes.
  </Banner>
  <Banner pal="sky" kicker="Double-tap the cover" title="Jump 10 seconds">
    Right side forward, left side back. Keep tapping to go 20, 30 seconds.
  </Banner>
</Row>

<Row>
  <Banner pal="pink" kicker="Pull up from the bottom" title="See what's next">
    Opens the queue. Drag a song by its handle to move it.
  </Banner>
  <Banner pal="orange" kicker="Drag down" title="Close the player">
    The music keeps playing in the mini player. Back works too.
  </Banner>
</Row>

The mini player swipes as well: sideways to change song, up to open the player.

## Wrong song? Pick another copy

Now and then the song that plays isn't the one you meant: a cover, a remix, a slowed version, or a different song with the same name. In the player, tap the small **source label** under the song name (or **⋮ → Wrong song?**).

<Banner pal="sky" kicker="Not the right song?" title="Swap it, and it stays swapped">
  You'll see the copy playing now, the other copies Relaxify already found, and more results, with a box to search yourself. Tap the right one and it takes over **at the same second**. From then on that song plays as your pick everywhere: albums, playlists, Liked Songs. The note that confirms it has **Undo**.
</Banner>

For a downloaded song, the note offers to **replace the download** with your pick.

## Lyrics that follow along

Tap **Lyrics** in the player. The line being sung lights up as the song plays, and tapping any line jumps the song there. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, so most popular songs have them; if a song has none, the tab says so.

## It never runs out

When your queue is about to end, **Autoplay** adds songs like the one you're hearing, so the music keeps going. Don't want that? Turn it off in **Settings → Autoplay**, and the suggested songs already waiting disappear too.

Long-press any song (or tap its **⋮**) for **Play next**, **Add to queue**, **Add to playlist**, **Download**, and a jump to its artist or album.

## Speed and sleep

In the player, the speed button plays from **0.25×** to **2×** without making voices sound squeaky. The timer button stops the music after **15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes**, or at the **end of this song**, fading out gently so it doesn't wake you.
