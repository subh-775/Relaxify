## One search, everywhere 🔍

Type a song, an artist, an album, or that one line stuck in your head. Relaxify checks JioSaavn and SoundCloud at once (and YouTube too, if you switch it on in **Settings → Sources**). Same song in three places? You see it once, and the other copies wait as backups in case one won't play.

The small label under each result says where it's from.

<Callout title="✨ Pro tip">
  Search also takes a **Spotify**, **YouTube** or **YouTube Music** playlist link and turns it into a Relaxify playlist. A friend's Relaxify playlist link works too. [How it works](/music#bring-your-playlists)
</Callout>

## Artist pages

Tap any artist's name to see their page: their photo, top songs and albums. **Play** starts their hits; **Follow** keeps them in Your Library.

## The player, one thumb 👍

Tap the bar at the bottom of the app to open the full player. Every move below also has a button, so do whatever feels easier.

<Moves>
  <Move how="Swipe the cover" emoji="👈">Left for the next song, right for the one before. Let go halfway and nothing changes.</Move>
  <Move how="Double-tap the cover" emoji="✌️">Right side jumps 10 seconds ahead, left side 10 back. Keep tapping to jump further.</Move>
  <Move how="Pull up" emoji="☝️">See what's playing next. Hold a song by its handle to move it around.</Move>
  <Move how="Drag down" emoji="👇">Close the player. The music keeps going in the bar.</Move>
</Moves>

The bar at the bottom works the same: swipe it sideways to change song, up to open the player.

## Wrong song?

😅 Got the slowed version, a cover, or a totally different song with the same name? Happens. In the player, tap the small **source label** under the song name (or **⋮ → Wrong song?**).

You'll see the copy playing now, other copies Relaxify found, and a search box. Tap one to try it: it starts from the same second. Tap **Use this one** and Relaxify remembers your pick everywhere, in every playlist and album. Changed your mind? The note has **Undo**.

## Lyrics that follow along 🎤

Tap **Lyrics** in the player. The line being sung lights up, and tapping any line jumps the song there. Most popular songs have them; if a song doesn't, the tab says so.

## The music never runs out

When your queue is about to end, **Autoplay** adds songs like the one you're hearing. Same when you tap a song from Recents or Search: similar songs follow. Not into that? Turn off **Settings → Autoplay**.

## Every song's menu

Press and hold any song (or tap its **⋮**) for **Play next**, **Add to queue**, **Add to playlist**, **Download**, and its artist or album.

## Speed and sleep 😴

The speed button plays from a quarter speed up to twice as fast, and voices don't go chipmunk. The timer button stops the music after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or when the current song ends, with a gentle fade so it won't wake you.
