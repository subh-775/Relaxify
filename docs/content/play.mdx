## One search, everywhere

Type a song, an artist, an album, or a line you remember. Relaxify searches JioSaavn and SoundCloud together, and YouTube as well if you turn it on in **Settings → Sources**. When a song is in more than one place, you see it once; the other copies are kept as spares, so if one won't play, the next one does.

A small label under each result says where it comes from. If a song sounds wrong, another copy is usually a tap away (see below).

<Callout title="Paste a link instead">
  Search also takes a **Spotify**, **YouTube** or **YouTube Music** playlist link, and turns it into a playlist for you. A shared Relaxify playlist link works too. [Bring your playlists](/music#bring-your-playlists)
</Callout>

## Artist pages

Tap an artist's name anywhere to open their page: their photo, their most popular songs, and their albums. **Play** starts their top songs; **Follow** keeps them in Your Library.

## The player

Tap the bar at the bottom of the screen to open the full player. Every gesture below also has a button, so use whichever feels easier.

<Moves>
  <Move how="Swipe the cover">Left for the next song, right for the previous one. Let go halfway and nothing changes.</Move>
  <Move how="Double-tap the cover">The right side jumps 10 seconds forward, the left side 10 seconds back. Keep tapping to jump further.</Move>
  <Move how="Pull up from the bottom">Shows what's playing next. Hold a song by its handle and drag it to move it.</Move>
  <Move how="Drag down">Closes the player. The music keeps going in the bar at the bottom.</Move>
</Moves>

The bar at the bottom works the same way: swipe it sideways to change song, or up to open the player.

## Wrong song? Pick another copy

Sometimes the song that plays isn't quite the one you wanted: a cover, a remix, a slowed version, or a different song with the same name. In the player, tap the small **source label** under the song name, or **⋮ → Wrong song?**

You'll see the copy playing now, the other copies Relaxify found, and a search box to look yourself. Tap one to listen; it starts from the same second. **Use this one** keeps it, and from then on that song always plays as your choice, in every playlist and album. The note that confirms it has **Undo**.

If you had downloaded the song, Relaxify offers to replace the download with your choice.

## Lyrics that follow along

Tap **Lyrics** in the player. The line being sung lights up, and tapping any line jumps the song there. Most popular songs have lyrics; when a song has none, the tab says so.

## It never runs out

When your queue is about to end, **Autoplay** adds songs like the one you're hearing, so the music doesn't stop. Songs from Recents and Search work the same way: tap one, and similar songs follow it. To stop that, turn off **Settings → Autoplay**; songs it had already lined up disappear too.

## Every song's menu

Press and hold any song, or tap its **⋮**, for **Play next**, **Add to queue**, **Add to playlist**, **Download**, and its artist or album.

## Speed and sleep

In the player, the speed button plays songs from a quarter of normal speed to twice as fast, and voices don't turn squeaky. The timer button is a sleep timer: it stops the music after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or when the current song ends, fading out gently.
