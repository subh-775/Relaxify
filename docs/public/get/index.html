Installing takes a minute

  1. Tap Download. The file is saved to your phone.
  2. Open the file from the notification, or from your Downloads folder.
  3. Tap Install. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from your browser: turn it on, go back, and tap Install.
  4. Open Relaxify and choose the languages you listen in.
Android says the app is from an unknown developer? That's because Relaxify isn't on the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

Already have it?

You don't need this page. Relaxify tells you when a new version is out and updates itself, keeping your playlists and likes. Please don't uninstall it to update: that deletes them.
